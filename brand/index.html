How to use the HumanityOne logo, color and type, and where to get the files.

Logo

Our logo is a family of three: the wordmark, the logomark and the One lockup. All three are drawn from the same idea, an O that stands on a pair of shoulders.

Wordmark

HumanityOne, set as one word with a capital H and a capital O. It's our primary signature and the first mark to reach for.

Logomark

The person on its own. Use it for app icons, avatars, favicons and anywhere too small for the name.

One lockup

A short signature for places where HumanityOne has already been introduced: a product name, a sub-brand, a second page.

Don't use it as the first or only mention of the brand.

Clear space

Keep everything outside the dashed line. The space on every side equals x, the height of the capital H, so it scales with the logo.

Do's and don'ts

The logo is only ever black or white, on a background with strong contrast. It's never redrawn, recolored or reshaped.

Legal variant

A version with the ™ symbol, for places that require a trademark notice: packaging, contracts, trademark filings and partner materials that ask for it.

Everywhere else, use the standard logo without ™.

Color

HumanityOne is black and white. Two colors used with conviction are easier to recognize than ten used politely.

Color scheme

Black and white are the whole brand palette. On screen our black is a hair softer than pure #000. In print, use 100% K.

Typography

Geist is the only typeface in the HumanityOne brand. Its mono companion handles numbers, specifications and code.
